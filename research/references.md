For every useful source, add:
- Title and URL
- Owner / project
- Branch, tag, commit, or document revision
- Date accessed
- What it establishes
- What it does **not** establish for pipa

Do not assume a reference for another Qualcomm device is directly compatible with Xiaomi Pad 6.
